# Architectural Decision Records

Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) document significant architecture and design choices made in CrossCodex, including context, options considered, decision rationale, and consequences.

**Status values**: `proposed` (under review), `accepted` (decided; the ADR says what is implemented), `deprecated` (superseded), `rejected` (decided against).
